# The Lantern Causeway

An observatory on a sea cliff opens its gates to the festival. Bronze lamp bowls lead toward a split gate and a stepped keep. Inside, a crimson runner and cold window light draw the visitor toward an empty volcanic-glass throne. The right arcade stays dark until the creature wakes.

| Chapter | Visitor objective | World response |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Follow the lamps from the causeway | The visitor can walk, turn and sprint. The parapets keep the path safe. |
| Gate | Inspect the doors and open them | Two leaves swing inward, dust rises at their feet and a low impact sounds when audio is enabled. |
| Hall | Follow the carpet to the obsidian throne | Repeated columns frame the dais. The next sculptures load while the visitor approaches. |
| Awakening | Witness the reveal | Ember eyes appear first. The camera eases toward the right; head, neck and upper chest emerge. The jaw opens, fire crosses the hall and a bright threshold dissolves the throne. Control returns as the plume fades. |
| Trophies | Inspect five golden objects | A chalice, split crown, faceted crystal, orrery and twisting beacon rise into view. Each opens one competition, its team size, provisional prize and registration action. The fifth also unlocks the main registration button. |

Movement has no combat, timers, damage or fail state. A revisit to a trophy preserves progress for the current walk. Replay returns the player to the reveal mark with discoveries intact. Skip is available once the sequence has been viewed in the session. The menu provides direct registration and access to all event information at any time. Audio is opt-in. Reduced-motion visitors default to the classic still experience.

The visual targets are the seven supplied film frames in `reference/`. The game translates the cold masonry, amber torches, one-sided shaft lighting and gold silhouettes into geometry and effects seen from a freely moving eye-level camera. The hero pieces are original Blender constructions. Event names, dates and track details are editable in `src/config.ts`.
